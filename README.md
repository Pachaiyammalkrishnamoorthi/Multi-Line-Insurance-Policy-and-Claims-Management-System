# Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System

## Overview

The **Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System** is a web-based application designed to manage insurance policies, customer information, and claims through a centralized platform.

The system helps users efficiently handle different types of insurance policies and provides a structured process for submitting, tracking, reviewing, and managing insurance claims.

## Features

* Customer registration and management
* Create and manage insurance policies
* Support for multiple insurance lines
* Policy status management
* Policy renewal tracking
* Insurance claim submission
* Claim tracking and management
* Claim approval and rejection
* Secure user authentication
* Centralized data management
* User-friendly and responsive interface

## Technologies Used

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** MongoDB
* **Authentication:** JWT
* **Tools:** VS Code, Git, GitHub, Postman

## System Workflow

1. User registers and logs into the system.
2. Customer details are added and managed.
3. Insurance policies are created and maintained.
4. Customers can submit insurance claims.
5. Claims are reviewed and processed.
6. The claim status is updated as approved or rejected.
7. Policy and claim information can be managed from the system.

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to provide a centralized and efficient solution for managing multiple insurance policies and claims. It reduces manual work, improves data organization, and makes the insurance management process easier and more transparent.

## Project Benefits

* Reduces manual documentation
* Improves policy and claim tracking
* Provides centralized information management
* Saves time and improves efficiency
* Makes claim processing more organized
* Provides easy access to policy and customer information
